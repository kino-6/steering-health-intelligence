Driver-Visible Pain

The strongest public pain pattern is not subtle degradation. It is driver-visible assist loss or increased steering effort, often worse at low speed or tied to warning indicators.

QuestionPublic data can showStill missing

What This Does Not Prove

This phase does not prove EPS failure prediction, diagnostic evidence value, NTF reduction, return-part analysis improvement, or OEM willingness to buy. It only makes public driver pain and context proxies visible.
